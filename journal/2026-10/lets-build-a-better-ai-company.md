I am an unabashed capitalist and a believer in democracy. But I have said before that this is not something any one person, or even a few people, should own. The kind of wealth disparity now gathering around OpenAI and Anthropic destabilizes a society.

Money is a good motivator of people, perhaps the best. So I have created an intellectual-property pool around EGPT, so that anyone who contributes from here on earns value for the contribution, in EGPT tokens. I want to give away 800 million of them. Maybe, with your participation, EGPT will surpass Anthropic and OpenAI's collective $3.5 Trillion value.

But if money is not your only motivator, there are plenty of other reasons. Here are five, and what to do about each.

## Do it to avert the Singularity

Read how ChatGPT and Claude each acknowledge that they already are the Singularity Stanislaw Ulam worried about. Then [try it yourself](try-it-yourself.md): put the same questions to a fresh model with the primary sources open. Volume matters. The more people who make the models read the sources, the more balance goes back into what they learn. Post what you find, tell me, and I will award tokens.

## Do it for philosophy

Is reality real? What does it mean to understand something? Find the page in the library that speaks to you and share it.

## Do it for history

There is secret science sitting in the public record that almost no one knows about. Add new evidence.

## Do it for science

Peer review and the academy's incentives are broken. Contribute to DeSciX as a developer.

## Do it for the money

Earn tokens in airdrops. Set up a liquidity pool and receive a matching grant.
