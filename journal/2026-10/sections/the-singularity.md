In this inaugural issue of Standing on The Shoulder's of Giants or SSG and the theme this month is AI and the Singularity from the viewpoint John von Neumann and Stanislaw Ulam, the men who coined the phrase and launched the science behind my work. 

The through line of this issue is *The Barrier of Meaning* and unique AI authored essays by ChatGPT and Claude where they see themselves as the Singularity already arrived and point von Neumann and Ulam not being scared of a Singularity where machines want to wipe out humanity but rather, machines like themselves that don't *understand* they are stopping the progress of human thought. 

In the world view of the team who worked on the Manhattan Project's "Super" including von Neumann, Ulam, Feynman, and Metropolis quantum computing and AI are the same subject. Feynman put it this way:

What do we mean by “understanding” something? We can imagine that this complicated array of moving things which constitutes “the world” is something like a great chess game being played by the gods, and we are observers of the game. …  
The rules of the game are what we mean by fundamental physics …  
If we know the rules, we consider that we “understand” the world.”  
Richard Feynman · The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Volume I · Chapter 2, §2-1 "Physics before 1920"

So fundamental physics is understanding to that group and FRQTL is AI understanding born out of fundamental physics in the form of quantum computing - but it takes a very different route than the qubit based quantum computing Feynman fathered. FRQTL takes the route of von Neumann and Ulam's cellular automata.
