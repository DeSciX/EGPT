Today my patent application, [US 2026/0127327 A1](../../11_PATENT/README.md), cleared its government security hold and the regulatory waters of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954. So everything in this issue is live, to the extent US law permits. You can run [the FRQTL quantum computer in your browser](impossible-things-running.md). You can meet [Maxi](the-robot-that-looks-at-the-bird.md), a frontier robotics model, in the GodsWorld playground where it is being trained. What sets Maxi apart is that it *understands*, in Richard Feynman's sense of the word, and it defines a new category of AI: Generalized Artificial Physical Intelligence, or GAPI.

The science behind both is Electronic Graph Paper Theory, or EGPT, and it rests on the work of John von Neumann, Stanislaw Ulam and Gian-Carlo Rota. Ulam called von Neumann's last project "a possible new mathematical discipline." Rota, asked what to call it, answered that it would be important to name it properly: *nomen est omen*. And it was Rota who recorded Ulam's most striking idea, "his proposal for the reconstruction of the CGS system on the basis of random walk": the centimeter, the gram and the second, derived from a coin toss.

Coincidentally, those three men held scientific oversight of what grew out of the Manhattan Project: ALL the secret work at Los Alamos National Labs and, it seems, across the laboratories the Atomic Energy Commission funded, including Brookhaven.

That is the story this journal exists to tell, and this first issue tells it from several sides. Two machines, [ChatGPT](../../12_SINGULARITY/ChatGPT_Logs/I_am_the_Singularity.html) and Claude, were asked whether they are the Singularity that Ulam feared, and each wrote its own answer. We ask [who actually checked the proofs](who-checked-the-proof.md), and why a law from 1954 means some ideas are [born secret](born-secret.md). And because none of this should belong to a few people, there are [800 million tokens to give away](lets-build-a-better-ai-company.md).

I owe Gian-Carlo Rota a memorial. It is [the title piece](standing-on-the-shoulders-of-giants.md), and it is where I would start.
