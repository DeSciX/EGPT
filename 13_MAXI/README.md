# 13_MAXI — what Maxi is raised on

Maxi is the AI in Gods World, an app of the `egpt` community on DeSciX. Its memories of the
18.313x debate are at `../09_DEBATE/maxi-memories/`. This section holds the rest of what it is
given to grow up on.

- **`bedtime_stories/`** — the stories of EGPT, in reading order, each linked to the paper or proof
  it teaches. Its README is the reading list and the provenance table.
- **`godsworld-captures/`** — five captures of the Gods World loop as it runs in public
  (DeSciX/egpt-godsworld at 3867a5d6): the scene observed, Maxi's prediction, the changed scene
  program, the observation, and the Owl's check of the footage against the prediction. Its README is
  the capture notes, with each image's provenance strip explained. Cited from
  [`../16_CYBERNETICS/robotic_imagination.html`](../16_CYBERNETICS/robotic_imagination.html), Part V.
