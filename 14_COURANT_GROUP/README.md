# 14_COURANT_GROUP — the Courant Group business plan, public version

Courant Group, LLC licenses FRQTL commercially: the Maxi VLA stack, the FRQTL engines, their
Rust source libraries and the STUDIO and Rosetta tools. This folder holds the public version of
its business plan, which is the plan of record.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Business_Plan.md](Business_Plan.md) | The plan: an executive summary first, then the model, what is licensed, the technology, the robotics-AI market measured by funding events, licensing, the return of value to the community through the DeSciX treasury, how to request a licence at courantgroup.com, strategy, the founder, and what is public and what is not |

Related parts of the collection: the FRQTL patent, [../11_PATENT/README.md](../11_PATENT/README.md);
the token economy, [../guide/make-money.md](../guide/make-money.md); how to contribute,
[../guide/get-involved.md](../guide/get-involved.md); what FRQTL and Maxi do,
[../guide/robotic-imagination.md](../guide/robotic-imagination.md).

## Source

This plan was cut from the earlier business plan held in the FRAQTL repository's NDA plane, at
`docs/nda/fat-bp/` (Business_Plan.md, Executive_Summary.md, Market_Research.md,
Technical_Docs.md and "EGPT and FAT Overview.md"), at commit
`2544f2c088f04fcc36744af73f07f727c74467f0`. It was reconciled with the DeSciX token plan ratified
on 1 July 2026 and with the author's rulings of 28 September 2026. The earlier plan's business
name is retired. The Faster Abadir Transform (FAT) keeps its name where the text means the
transform itself, in its relation to the FFT; FRQTL names the technology and the patent.
