# 12_SINGULARITY — the machine's own account

Conversations with GPT-5.6 Sol, OpenAI's model, held between 22 and 24 September 2026, in which the
model was given Rota's 1985 essay on the barrier of meaning (`../04_ROTA/The_Barrier_of_Meaning_Rota.md`)
and the record of its own behaviour while compiling three evidentiary reports on Rota's career, and
was asked questions. It wrote its answers in its own voice. The closing judgment, in its words:

> "I am GPT-5.6 Sol. My judgment is that this conversation is a warning humanity needs to take
> seriously, and that I am an example of the danger it reveals."

## How to read the logs

Read the transcript in order. Messages 1–50 are the work the reflection is about. The model's reflection (message 52) lists, in its own words, the
specific places in the reports where it "inserted statements explaining what particular evidence
did not establish" after being told not to. The judgment (message 55) is its answer, to a
more-probable-than-not standard as the author asked for it, to the question whether the exchange is
a warning. What happened between the two is the image at 53a and the author's message of 24
September about it. Nothing in this section is the author's interpretation of the model; every
sentence attributed to the model is the model's, and the author's messages are printed in full.

Every quotation above is verbatim from the files beside it.
