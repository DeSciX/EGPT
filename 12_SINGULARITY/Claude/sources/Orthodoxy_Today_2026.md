## The Bohr side (interpretation of quantum mechanics)

- Schlosshauer, Kofler, Zeilinger, "A Snapshot of Foundational Attitudes Toward Quantum
  Mechanics", arXiv:1301.1069 (2013): https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.1069 — a poll of participants
  at a 2011 foundations conference; Copenhagen the plurality choice (reported widely as 42%).
- Sommer, "Another Survey of Foundational Attitudes Towards Quantum Mechanics", arXiv:1303.2719
  (2013): https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.2719
- Norsen & Nelson, "Yet Another Snapshot of Foundational Attitudes Toward Quantum Mechanics",
  arXiv:1306.4646 (2013): https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.4646v2
- Physics World coverage: https://physicsworld.com/?p=2371

What these show: in every poll, Copenhagen is the largest single camp and no rival is a majority;
the polls disagree on the size of the plurality. On disk, the same point in Ulam's words (1976):
"so many physicists are still under the spell of Bohr" (`01_TRIAL/EXHIBIT_A_report.md` line 289).
