# The Polymath Project (2009), filed under rule 10

Filed 1 October 2026 (UTC) by the self-assessment session, in answer to the author's question
whether any prior process recorded and showed all its work, including failed attempts, on a
problem of magnitude. Read 1 October 2026 via web search; not read in full.

- Wikipedia, "Polymath Project": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath_Project — Timothy Gowers's
  January 2009 blog experiment; Polymath1 proved a combinatorial Density Hales–Jewett theorem
  through public blog comments, every contribution and dead end left in the open thread.
- Gowers & Nielsen, "Massively collaborative mathematics", Nature 461, 15 October 2009:
  https://michaelnielsen.org/papers/mcm.pdf
- MAA news, 28 October 2009: https://www.maa.org/news/math-news/big-project-proves-many-hands-do-not-spoil-math-broth
- Scientific American, "Problem Solved, LOL": https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/problem-solved-lol/

What it is for this assessment: a precedent for publishing the whole working record, failed
attempts included, on a serious open problem. What it is not: a Millennium-magnitude claim, a
Lean formalization, or an adversarial debate with recorded concessions.
