# DAITA: A Sustainable Data Economy For Artificial Intelligence

Essam E Abadir, SB, SB, JD

September 25, 2023

## Abstract

A sustainable economic model for Open Source is needed and DAITA aims to provide that in the form of Community Source - a decentralized community that anyone can join, everyone who joins has economic ownership in, and gaining access can be done through contributing to the community, or, by buying in with fiat currency. Open Source addresses Somethings are so foundational to progress and development that no single person should own them and everyone should be able to realize the economic benefits, however, this must be balanced with a capitalist incentive to protect and reward creators. History has shown that creators benefit most when their work is adopted and enhanced by a thriving community. Previously, there was no trackable and enforceable way to implement an economic framework to warp around open source, now, with blockchain there is. DAITA is a framework that leverages blockchain and crypto currency as a tracking mechanism for intellectual property in order to enable the Community Source model that gives economic structure around Open Source.

As AI proves to be the single biggest paradigm shift in Human evolution, it’s voracious demands for information are likewise proving that data is the single most valuable commodity. Data and intellectual property are fed to generative AI to beget new data and intellectual property (data + AI = “Daita”) in a perpetual cycle. How we value anything like land, health, or a job is entirely dependent on what we know about it like zoning/weather, MRI showing a tumor, or what other employers pay. In these terms, daita should be the new digital gold standard. Daita is encompassed under the legal frameworks of intellectual property and AI’s now typically quantify data in terms of “tokens” – a mathematical measure of the information in the data. Accordingly, Daita Tokens are proposed as an open, digital, and cryptographically secure monetary system for trading in the Daita commodity. The proposed Daita currency aims to address the following issues:

- Compensating creators whose data feeds AI, such as, open-source developers, writers of books, philosophers, or even Reddit commentors.
- Enabling data sharing while maintaining privacy and provable authenticity.

This Whitepaper:

1. Introduces the Daita Token, economy, and assets.
2. Describes the simulation model for the Daita economy
3. Overviews the personas, protocols, and core components.
4. Frames governance and integrity procedures for the Daita token, chain, and economy.

BACKGROUND:

**The Demand For Data & Market Size:**

In the near term, how will creators get paid when people turn to an AI chatbot instead of visiting a web site or reading book? Meanwhile, owners of AI’s seek to train them on every book, website, and scientific paper. AI’s data demands grow 10X in every generation making Moore’s Law look positively quaint by comparison: GPT3 was trained on 175 billion parameters (45TB) , GPT4 1.75 trillion parameters, GPT5 likely 17.5 trillion, etc..

At the same time, generative AI holds the key to future “miracles” ranging from cancer cures to things we have yet to imagine. This generative AI is even creating new information at an astonishing rate. For example, protein fold structures are crucial to drug development. Through 2021 decades of PhD research only discovered 200,000 protein structures. In the following two years an AI called AlphaFold discovered 100X as many protein sequences as all PhD’s in human history. AlphaFold was of course trained with the 200,000 folds discovered by humans and the chemistry knowhow of decades of PhD research. Will those PhD’s or research institutions get compensated? What is the value of the 200 million protein folds to the drugs which later get developed? On the one hand, if the 200,000 weren’t shared with AlphaFold the incredible advancement would never have come about, on the other this is an incredible disincentive to “creators” who won’t get value for sharing their ideas – whether those ideas are PhD research or books. Even now the news is littered with stories detailing rampant tension between creators and AI training.

# The Daita Token Economy (DTE)

## I. Introduction

### A. Technology

There are well known conflicts and concerns between “creators” and workers on one-side and generative AI companies on the other. Each week seemingly brings high profile news items like the Screen Writers and UAW strikes. A publicly licensable and trackable pool of data and intellectual property would have many in the coming AI age. For example, if a generative AI were trained using a screen play, then the screen play writer should economically benefit from that AI’s created works. Similarly, the work done by an auto worker contributes to the data an IP of an auto company and the future innovations driven by AI should benefit that auto-worker in proportion to their contribution.

How can we track data and IP contributions? How can we provide an auditable framework for licensing? Can we preserve privacy and prove data authenticity? Are their securities law implications? To these issues and more, we propose the Daita Token Economy (DTE) as a solution. At root, the DTE has one foot in modern math and algorithmic techniques like blockchains and simulations and another in traditional legal structures such as IP licenses and tax reporting. implements the fundamental principles of classical micro-economic systems. At base The economy is enabled by AI developers such as OpenAI or Google purchasing access to the data and IP by gaining Daita Tokens, where Daita Tokens are initially minted to the data creators. That is, creators gain Daita Tokens in exchange for their data and IP contributions. Conversely, AI developers, such as OpenAI or Google, desiring access to the pool of IP and data must both consent to real-world license requiring resale royalties as well as purchase Daita Tokens from token holders (initially the creators) in order to exchange those tokens for data and IP access.

The DTE is designed to support a nearly limitless number of use cases, or smart contracts in blockchain parlance, which build off data and IP. Without intending to limit the scope of potential smart contracts which can be developed to support as yet un-imagined use cases supportable by the Daita Token Economy, this paper walks through the foundational smart contract use case of creators reviews on the Daita Token Economy.

Fake data and deep fakes made by AI are an inherently difficult problem which must be accounted for. Addressing deep fake related shortcomings requires techniques uniting strong privacy protections, data security, and novel mechanisms authenticating each data contribution’s unique link to an actual, preceding, data event. In other words, each data record and matched review must be Authentic, Unique, and Private.

We created The DTE utility token (hereinafter, the “Daita token”) to transform closed data and IP silos, using legacy technologies, into public, auditable, blockchains to provide for an extensible, sustainable, and efficient economy.

### B. Community Source & Digital Smart Contracts: IP Licensing & Tracking

The reader may have already noticed that the Daita Token Economy aims to create a hybrid between “Open Source” where the public has free access to a pool of intellectual property (IP) such as source code, data, images, song lyrics, etc. and commercial entities which rely on private contracts such as end user license agreements to sell access to the IP. The complexity of monetizing Open Source and the tensions with commercial licensing are well known and go beyond the scope of this whitepaper.

For our purposes, it suffices to focus on mandatory tax reporting required by commercial activity and essential role that corporate entities have historically played by do the generally inefficient accounting that goes along with paper tracking of commercial licenses.

The DTE solves this tension via a real world foundation entity and digital autonomous organization (DAO):

- Real World Contracts: There is a legal entity or community contract which empowers real world individuals to (the “Foundation") which tracks compliance with jurisdictional regulations such as real world licenses, know your customer laws, etc. and support the operation of the network.
- Digital Smart Contracts: The foundation auditable tracks contractual responsibilities using digital smart contracts which put an audit trail to a block-chain.

## II. Personas and Roles in the Daita Token Economy: Healthcare As An Example

For clarity, the remainder of the technical description uses the healthcare sector as a reference implementation for the DTE. The DTE is in no way limited to healthcare data or IP, however, healthcare is a known hard sector in terms of regulation, data privacy issues, and monetization. Hence, the choices of healthcare as the exemplary sector may make illuminate more fully the implementation choices in the DTE.

The DTE token and economy are based upon standard cryptographic and blockchain methodologies, with a fixed number of mining nodes and potentially unlimited developers offering services and assets in return for Daita Tokens. Thus, in this economy, we define the following personas, roles, and activities (Refer also to the *Glossary of Terms*):

1. **Stakeholders (SH):** Stakeholders are any entity holding Daita Tokens. Stakeholders include Miners, Data Providers, Creators, Node Operators, Developers, Delegates, and others listed below.
2. **Witness-Validators (WV):** Witness-Validators are responsible for mining Daita Tokens. As published and administered by the Foundation, WV are reviewed by the Foundation and must meet regulatory requirements in their sector. For example, in healthcare WV must meet stringent requirements for integrating with ONC Health IT certified (23) electronic medical record systems (EMR), and protocols for data privacy, integrity, and security. WV retrieve Electronic Data (DAITA) records from data providers and administer Patient Surveys (PS). DAITA and PS records are synthesized into Private, Authentic, and Unique Creator Data and IP (PRev), and are recorded on the chain.
3. **Data Providers (HP):** Physicians and other data providers receive a portion of the Daita Tokens mined by WV by maintaining certified EMR systems and submitting their DAITA data to WV for validation and mining. HP can utilize these Daita Tokens for review and reputation assets (e.g., Star Ratings on practice websites), practice analytics, and similar data resources.
4. **Creators (P)**: Creators receive a portion of the Daita Tokens mined by WV by responding to data contribution invitations and completing creator reviews. Creators utilize earned Daita Tokens searching for other HP and datas.
5. **Foundation Members (FM):** Foundation members are elected by those SH who hold Governance Tokens, described below. Elected FM supervise participation, regulation, operation, efficiency, and integrity for the Daita Token Economy. The Foundation oversees use of Daita Tokens received during mining and redemption to maintain and expand the Daita Token Economy, and engaging Delegates to assist in these tasks. At least 60% of serving Foundation members may not have any other role described in this section during their time on the Foundation.
6. **Delegates (DG):** Delegates are appointed or engaged by FM on an *ad hoc* basis. Delegates receive Daita Tokens in return for providing services (e.g., supervision, data-integrity, integration, connectivity, optimization, audit services, etc.) that benefit the Daita Token Economy.
7. **Node Operators (NO):** Node Operators create and maintain resources, infrastructure, and computational assets required to support search, providing analytic tools and assets, and other functions required for purchasing, trading, or redeeming Daita Tokens in the Daita Token Economy. Node Operators receive transaction fee based Daita Tokens when the Daita Tokens are utilized within the function fulfilled by the NO. For example, a search enabling NO would receive a stake proportionate share of tokens related to API services and join the economy after certification by FM and their DG.
8. **Developers (DV):** Developers create and maintain smart contracts and Distributed Applications (Dapps) which natively support DAITA as the central bank for the ERC-20 token of the DApp - that is the DApp may have its own virtual currency but the minting,treasury, and other functions are through the DAITA DAO smart contract. Developers receive Daita Tokens in addition to setting their initial money supply when their DApp joins the economy after certification by FM and their DG.
9. **Treasury (T):** The Treasury operates in the traditional role of a central bank treasury. Daita Tokens held by the Treasury, outside active circulation, and The DTE money supply function turns to these Treasury tokens in preference to creating new Daita Tokens for the economy. Then Treasury supplies are depleted, the money function create additional Daita Tokens as allowed by the economy.
10. Future Role Opportunities for the Daita Token Economy:
    1. **Referrers (RF):** Referrers are adjuncts to WV, creating connectors and integration with certified EMR systems, insurance payers, or self-insured employers’ claim systems. RF would receive a share of WV Daita Tokens for the EMR records provided that are successfully recorded on chain as DAITA. At THE DTE launch, WV will serve as their own RF.
    2. **Resellers (RS):** Resellers are adjuncts to DV, as aggregators and bulk-sellers of search, computational, and Dapps. RS negotiate directly with DV for token sharing when DV receive Daita Tokens assets sold by the RS.

## III. Market Efficiency Protocols

The core premise of The DTE token economy is based upon the *Efficient Market Hypothesis*, where informational (datas and reviews) inefficiencies are captured in token value, via an algorithmic, self-regulating, central-banking, money supply algorithm. Based upon a *constant product market maker* algorithm, a hypothetically “perfectly efficient” market will transfer fractional value into Daita Tokens in two ways:

1. **Earn:** New data and creator review data are captured informational inefficiency is reduced;
2. **Redeem:** Past data and creator review data are searched revealing the value of these data.

Within our economy, at time of “Redeem” activity, the algorithm shall decide whether to remove Daita Tokens from circulation (re-allocation into Treasury) creating incentives for miners and other Daita token-holding stakeholders in the economy. The Daita Token Economy includes a theoretical limit of total Daita Tokens in supply. For all minting activities, the following principles apply: a) New data are more highly valued; b) If the theoretical limit of Daita Tokens in the economy has been reached, the Treasury serves to continue incentivizing addition of new data.

The Foundation and their Delegates will be tasked with identifying the algorithmic control schemas employed to optimize, a hybrid version(24) of the Constant Product ( x\*y=c ) Constant Function ( x+y=c ) market maker algorithms have previously been successfully deployed in cryptocurrency applications, and would serve to achieve the goals outlined above, while protecting the marketplace from surges and swings in volume and value. ## V. Governance:

1. Stakeholders (WV, RF, P, HP, NO, and DV) actively involved in mining and redemption activities are issued special Governance Tokens (GT). GT are not issued to governing (Foundation, DG), redeeming, or non-participatory (speculators, etc.) Daita token holders. GT provide Stakeholders with representative governance of the economy, mitigating 51% (and other) perils that speculative investors might attempt to exploit. With the exception of NO, GT are issued either in proportion to the number of Daita Tokens mined by the SH listed above during the previous calendar year. NO must acquire a minimum of 5% stake of issued Daita Tokens in order to be granted a GT. GT carry the following roles and restrictions:
    1. GT are used as Stakeholder voting tokens for electing Foundation members, and for voting on Foundation member and Delegate recommendations for administration of the economy;
    2. GT cannot be traded or exchanged;
    3. GT are returned to the Treasury at the end of the calendar year of issuance.
2. Elected Foundation Members (EFM) elected by GT votes constitute three of the five members of the Foundation board and may not have simultaneous activity in any other Role (described above) during their time of Foundation service. The Foundation Members (FM) collectively supervise and evaluate WV efficiency with recommendations for retention in the economy; monitor transactional capacity and throughput; oversee innovation of new tools and assets; and remedy of technical problems facing the Daita token and blockchain. FM adjudicate any perceived breaches of authenticity, uniqueness or privacy, and conduct any audit activities and interventions required to preserve integrity of the Daita token and economy. FM utilize Daita Tokens allocated as part of mining and redemption activities in support of these activities.
3. Delegates (DG) are *ad hoc* appointees of the FM serving in purely advisory and supportive roles, without governance authority, serving at the pleasure of the Foundation. DG may be recruited from commercial, regulatory, and academic organizations. NO are a special case of DG where the NO must acquire a minimum of 5% stake to be considered trustworthy by the network before EFM may appoint them as Delegates. DG receive Daita Tokens in return for services provided to the Foundation, using Daita Tokens allocated in part B above. The Foundation is solely responsible for defining the scope of DG services and compensation.
4. It is the Foundation’s responsibility to determine administration criteria for participation as WV, RF, NO and DV. Specifically, each role requires compliance with published criteria for participation. Examples include, but are not limited to:
    1. WV demonstrate technical, procedural, and security capabilities to receive EMR data and transact PS;
    2. RF demonstrate HIPAA-compliant integration with EMR systems, and technical and security protocols compliant with PoU and PoP protocols;
    3. NO provide service and search resource platforms with demonstrable scale and capacity to meet consumer demands, as well as the ability to provide liquidity sufficient to buffer the economy from volume-based shocks;
    4. DV create Dapp/DApp assets that meet the statistical requirements defined by the Foundation and render these assets in manner compliant with requirements of the economy.
