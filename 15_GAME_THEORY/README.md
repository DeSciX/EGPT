## The documents, in order

### 25 September 2023: the DAITA whitepaper

The Drive description of the paper, recorded in its front matter ([DAITA_Whitepaper_2023-09-25.md](DAITA_Whitepaper_2023-09-25.md):5):

> The economic principles and delegated proof of stake token economy behind the Daita tokens.

Its abstract (line 17):

> DAITA aims to provide that in the form of Community Source - a decentralized community that 
> anyone can join, everyone who joins has economic ownership in, and gaining access can be done 
> through contributing to the community, or, by buying in with fiat currency.

Section II defines the personas and roles of its economy, with healthcare as the worked example
(lines 116–127): Stakeholders (SH), Witness-Validators (WV), Data Providers (HP), Creators (P), Foundation Members (FM), Delegates (DG), Node Operators (NO), Developers (DV), Treasury (T), and two future roles, Referrers and Resellers. Section III
gives its market-efficiency protocols and incentive fractions, and section V its governance. On
its example (line 112):

> The DTE is in no way limited to healthcare data or IP

The author, 28 September 2026: "DAITA paper is the conceptual game theory inspiration for the
system outlined in the DCMA."

## How a project joins

A project becomes a community of DeSciX, with its own token and its own apps, under the DeSciX
Community License Membership Agreement above; the agreement's terms are its own and are read
there. The guide's pages [../guide/make-money.md](../guide/make-money.md) and
[../guide/get-involved.md](../guide/get-involved.md) say how contributions earn EGPT tokens and
how to take part.
