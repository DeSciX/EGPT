## Why it is here

The update letter in `07_LETTERS/` states that one of the author's patent applications sat under
a publication hold for security review for over a year. This is that application, now published,
and publication is what makes it citable. The abstract describes predictive physics compression:
physical systems represented as hierarchical frames in a universal grid, advanced in discrete
time steps by traversing the frame hierarchy, distributable across a network. That is the
mechanism the FRQTL engine implements.

The January 2025 letter offering the token gift, which concerns this application, is not
reproduced in this collection. The publication is the citation of record.
