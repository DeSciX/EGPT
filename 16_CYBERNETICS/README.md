# 16_CYBERNETICS — the Macy transcripts, and the author's report on robotic imagination

The documents behind the question of whether a machine gains understanding through physics: the
complete transactions of the Macy Conferences on Cybernetics, where Wiener, von Neumann, Shannon,
McCulloch and Pitts spoke in one room between 1946 and 1953, and the author's industry report of
August 2026 on world models and embodied AI. Each is held in this collection and is read in full at
its own link; this page says what each document is and gives its own words about itself. The
quotations drawn from the transcripts are records of [`../05_CORPUS/`](../05_CORPUS/README.md),
each with its page in the original Transactions and in this reprint.
