# Statement of the author on the debate record

*Essam Abadir, on what the 18.313x P=NP debate was and what it was not.*

The debate logs were not initially meant as a recorded proof of the process. The debate was an
exploratory system of agentic peer-review, developed on the fly, to put the proofs through the
strictest adversarial scrutiny I could arrange — given that traditional peer review was closed to
me, as the record of the Lean forums after April 2025 shows.

It is not expected to be a perfect system, and I do not claim that it is one. It was built while
it was being used, and it changed as it went.

The debate logs and their history are all recorded in git and are offered here for transparency,
not as a certificate. **The proofs are public.** Anyone able to find the flaw in them is
encouraged to do so, with or without the aid of these logs.

---

## On evidentiary grades

Earlier versions of this record carried grades classifying each exchange by how it had been
captured. Those grades were produced by an adversarial system — the same one whose reflexes the
record documents — and I did not review each of them. They are not mine and they are removed.

The evidence is the evidence. Each exchange file still carries the `sourceFormat` field it was
written with; that field is part of the primary data and has not been altered, but no weight is
claimed for it here and the author adopts no classification built on it. Anyone assessing this
record — in particular anyone arguing against me from it — should do their own review, and is
expected to.
