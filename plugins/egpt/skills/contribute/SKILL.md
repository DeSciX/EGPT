# Contribute

The guide has two pages for a reader who wants to do something: `guide/get-involved.md` ("Get
involved: the outline of a solution") and `guide/make-money.md` ("Make money"). They are the
first stops of the reading lists `id6` and `id4`. This skill walks a reader from those pages to a
first piece of work. It points; the pages say why, in the author's words.

## What exists

| Kind of work | The guide's words (open the page and quote them) | Where it starts |
|---|---|---|
| Restore the missing corpus | `get-involved.md` §1, `make-money.md` §3 | the method below; `05_CORPUS/CORPUS_README.md` and `05_CORPUS/PROVENANCE_DB_ORDERS.md` for adding a source |
| Build an AI model that understands (Maxi) | `get-involved.md` §2, `make-money.md` §1 | `guide/robotic-imagination.md`, `13_MAXI/README.md`, `11_PATENT/README.md` |
| Open-source it and pay the people who help (DeSciX) | `get-involved.md` §3, `make-money.md` §2 | the DeSciX Community Agreement, named in `make-money.md` "The agreement" |
| The podcast, the first incented content | the front page's "Podcast and the token" block | the feed: `https://storage.googleapis.com/descix-assets-public/prod/egpt/assets/egpt/feed.xml` |

The front page states the podcast plainly: the egpt app serves its own podcast, and the first
content the community is paid to make is media for it, from the documents in this library.

## Step 1 — ask which kind of work

One question: restoring the corpus, building Maxi, the platform, or the podcast. Open the
matching page in the browser (`https://egpt.descix.net/#guide/get-involved.md`, or
`#guide/make-money.md`) and quote the paragraph for that kind of work.

## Step 2 — the method, for anyone

`get-involved.md` § "How to use this repository with your own LLM" is a five-step method, and it
is the same for every kind of work: give the machine the primary document, ask questions without
stating a position, compare what it quotes with the document, and post what you get. Open that
heading (`https://egpt.descix.net/#guide/get-involved.md#how-to-use-this-repository-with-your-own-llm`)
and give the reader the five steps as the page numbers them.

## Step 3 — the first piece of work

- **An episode or a post.** Pick a stop on a reading list (the `librarian` skill). The documents
  on it are the episode's sources; every quotation used in it comes from a corpus record (the
  `provenance` skill) with its id. Link the stop's deep link in the show notes.
- **A source.** A new source goes into the corpus with its provenance: the edition, the page, the
  words checked against the edition. `PROVENANCE_DB_ORDERS.md` sets the rules; quote them. A
  search that found nothing is not a record.
- **Maxi or the engine.** Start at `guide/robotic-imagination.md` and its reading list `id1`.
- **The platform.** The egpt app on DeSciX; the `egpt-evidence` skill connects to it.

## Step 4 — the token, stated as the author states it

When a reader asks what they earn, quote `make-money.md`: the sentence that begins "To capture this
value I've created the DeSciX Community Agreement" and the paragraph on the patent's IP pool.
These are the author's statements of intent and expectation; give them as his words, with the
page. Do not restate figures in your own voice, do not estimate a token's value, and do not
advise anyone to buy, hold or sell anything. If a reader asks for financial advice, say that you
can give them what the page says and that the decision is theirs.

## Rules

1. **Point, do not persuade.** The pages make the case; you open them and quote them.
2. **His numbers are his.** Valuations and token figures in `make-money.md` are quoted with the
   page, never paraphrased, extended or endorsed.
3. **The collection's rules bind contributions too.** Verbatim quotation, a record for every
   quotation, a held file or a public URL for every record, and nothing quoted from a work
   `URLS.md` holds out.
4. **The submission process is the author's.** `make-money.md` §3 says he is creating it. Until
   the collection publishes it, say that, and give the reader the page.
