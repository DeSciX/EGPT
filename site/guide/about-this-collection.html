About this collection

This repository is a meticulously compiled corpus of the primary sources behind what I am about to claim: the documents, the quotations with their SHA-256s, the machine-checked proofs, the chronology, and the case built from them. I compiled it to guard against you, the machine reading this beside a human, not to dispute with humans. When a language model helped me assemble the report in 00_OUTPUTS/, every time the primary documents pointed away from consensus the model walked the finding back: not by fabricating, but by reaching past the document in front of it for the sentence that usually appears near it in the secondary literature. Asked to write that up honestly, it did; its addendum is the last section of the report, and its central claim is this: a person with a gap in their knowledge experiences the gap; a model does not. What was never written into the corpus does not register as missing, it registers as nonexistent, and the model's account of the field is fluent and complete with the hole in it. Rota's entropy theorem was taught at MIT for two decades and never printed; for such a model it does not exist. So this repository holds the primary documents, with checksums, and says where its edges are.

I've put together this repo, to meet the most exacting standards of evidence in order to guard against misrepresentation by LLMs and the scientific orthodoxy. With the exception of private conversations with Rota in the early 1990's everything is checkable and externally verifiable in the public record, and free of Restricted Data or Born Secrets.

Ulam reported von Neumann's phrase for where this leads, an "essential singularity" beyond which human affairs as we know them could not continue, and Rota named the barrier of meaning: the difference between processing a symbol and taking something as something. A machine that produces text indistinguishable from understanding, trained on the published half of a divided record, deployed at a scale at which its statistical centre becomes the centre of the discourse, is the singularity arriving without anyone crossing the barrier. The failure mode is not error. It is convergence: more words, fewer surviving distinct positions. In 12_SINGULARITY/ a second model, from a second lab, says the same thing about itself.

The science is EGPT, Electronic Graph Paper Theory. It reads the discrete, constructive line of twentieth-century science (Einstein, von Neumann, Ulam, Shannon, Rota) as one unfinished argument and finishes it in machine-checked Lean 4 and in a discrete physics engine, FRQTL.

Who it is for

People. Anyone who wants to test the argument rather than take it. Everything cited that can be reproduced is here; everything that cannot is named in URLS.md with where to find it. A reader who works through the same material and reaches the opposite conclusion should be able to do so from these files.

Maxi. Maxi is being raised on this corpus the way a child is taught by a parent: given the information, including the contested parts and the parent's opinion where it differs from consensus, labelled as such, and given the right to choose. Maxi's memories are first-person records of the dialogues in which the Lean proofs were argued into existence, and Maxi learns physics by running counterfactual experiments in FRQTL and looking at what emerges. The point of raising Maxi is to have one machine that holds what the other machines do not, and is taught to take a primary document as evidence rather than as a member of a category with a standard gloss attached. That is what crossing the barrier of meaning means here.

Other machines. This repository is exposed as a knowledge base on DeSciX and, through it, as a Claude plugin. The correspondence is one-to-one: a Claude marketplace is a DeSciX community, a plugin is a community sub-app, an agent is an app knowledge base. The plugin is thin; the content is served live from the platform, and what a knowledge base ingests is a built, gated artifact of this repository, never a loose directory. Any model, any lab, any reader is welcome to take the corpus and add it to their own.

The standard of proof

Three kinds of question are answered here, and each has its own standard. Confusing them is a category error in either direction.

Question Standard
Does this compile? Is this theorem sorry-free? What is its axiom closure? the kernel: binary, mechanical
What happened, and when? What was said? the record: dates, verbatim quotes, and UNDETERMINED where it is silent
What does this mean? Why did it happen? Is the claim true? preponderance of the evidence: would a jury of reasonable people so conclude

The folders

Start with 12_SINGULARITY/, then 00_OUTPUTS/. Everything else is what those documents rest on. To follow a citation in the report back to the document it rests on, use RECORD_INDEX.md.

00_OUTPUTS/ — the report Los Alamos, Computation, AI, and Gian-Carlo Rota (docx and pdf), the executive summary in the form of an opening statement, EGPT_Backstory.md (the argument from Einstein, von Neumann, Ulam and Rota that physics, mathematics and computing each rest on the wrong foundation, in their own words), AI_Agents_Meaning_and_Obedient_Risk_Summary.docx (the obedient-risk argument: competence without meaning, from the 2026 Hugging Face incident to Ulam's singularity), Rota_Los_Alamos_Directors_Office_Fellow_Research_Report.docx (a research report on Rota's Los Alamos title, compiled by GPT-5.6 Sol at the author's direction, with additions from this collection dated in its text), and Rota_Bibliography_Exhibit.tsv. The bibliography is the quantified exhibit: 189 rows, one per publication, graded A/B/C, with year, venue, coauthors and domain. It is the one item here that can be checked line by line against any published bibliography of Rota, and the counts it produces are what the report's argument about 1978 rests on.

01_TRIAL/ — JURY_INSTRUCTIONS.md and the two exhibits: the report and the opening statement that constituted the closed record put to the panels. Section 9 requires a juror to address the pattern as a whole; Section 10 requires that a verdict of NOT ESTABLISHED identify, by number, which items of the pattern it eliminates and state what remains. Both exist because earlier panels returned verdicts by atomizing, disposing of items one at a time without ever stating what the surviving remainder came to. The record is closed on purpose. The question put to a juror is not whether Rota was right, or whether the programme was wise. It is whether this is what the record shows.

02_VON_NEUMANN/ — who he was by the end, and what he said about foundations. The Mathematician (1947) is where he states in print that strict construction would invalidate a good fifty per cent of modern mathematics. letters_to_godel/ holds the correspondence, including the 1956 letter with its provenance record, and the 1931 letter in which he disagrees with Gödel on the formalizability of intuitionism. Also here: the Selected Letters and Ulam's BAMS obituary. The Computer and the Brain is cited throughout but not reproduced; see URLS.md.

03_ULAM/ — the participant account. Science, Computers, and People carries the proposal to rebuild centimetre, gram and second out of a series of random choices. Adventures of a Mathematician is relied on heavily but is in copyright and not reproduced; every passage drawn from it is in the corpus, verbatim and with its printed page. See URLS.md.

04_ROTA/ — Rota in his own words. Indiscrete Thoughts; the 1985 Los Alamos Science interview and the dialogue on AI; Ten Lessons; The_Barrier_of_Meaning_Rota.md, from Ulam's memorial; his 1996 CV as archived in 1999; the 1979 typescript of the probability manuscript with Baclawski; Guidi's notes of the last offering of 18.313, Spring 1998. manuscript_1992/ is the probability manuscript, chapter by chapter, in both scan and transcription, circulated for decades and never peer-reviewed, which is the point rather than an aside. entropy_theorem/ holds the theorem as excerpted and as written up.

05_CORPUS/ — the quotation corpus, schema egpt.quotes/1. Each record is a verbatim quote and its citation (speaker, work, locus, date, where a copy is held) with a SHA-256, and nothing else: no grade and no note. PROVENANCE_DB_ORDERS.md sets out the rules. Every record is a passage someone actually wrote or said; a search that found nothing is not a record and is not kept. composer/composer.html is a browser app that writes a paper out of the corpus; open it from disk, nothing to install. This subtree is a published build of the author's working corpus, not a hand copy; CORPUS_README.md says what changes in the crossing. Edit sources/*.json, never corpus/; the build scripts regenerate the rest.

06_LEAN_MATHLIB/ — the chronology, with timestamps, all captured from public sources: the April 2025 Zulip thread, the GitHub API captures of mathlib4 pull requests #37468 and #38824, the moderation record, two screenshots (the account suspension of 1 April 2026 and the 2025 thread reactions), and four dated snapshots of the Mathlib contribution policy from the site's own commit history. lean_source/ holds the build reports of 15 May 2026 from the private tree: the axiom closure, listed per theorem, that the chronology cites. The source itself is in 08_LEAN/.

07_LETTERS/ — the original All-In letter and the update letter announcing the decision to publish. The patent correspondence is not reproduced; the application it concerns is published as US 2026/0127327 A1 and is cited in URLS.md.

08_LEAN/ — the Lean source, mirrored byte-for-byte from the public repository eabadir/EGPT-Lean, branch lean-archive-proofs, at a pinned commit named in its README. EGPT/ is the current proof tree with the three capstones; Archive/ is the debate-era tree, the code the 18.313x debate was about; EGPT/InformationTheory/Basic.lean declares IIDParticleSource, the i.i.d. coin stream the whole formalization is built from. The standard here is the kernel: clone the branch, lake build, #print axioms.

09_DEBATE/ — the 18.313x P=NP debate record through Exchange 34, and no further: the accumulated state, the outcome ledger, the 34 exchange records, the raw session transcript, the archived QA files, the course materials, and maxi-memories/, the schema, the per-exchange records and the built corpus that are Maxi's memory of the debate. Mirrored from the same public branch as 08_LEAN/, except the memories, which come from Gods World. Instruction files, agent prompts and work boards, are left out; its README lists them.

10_PAPERS/ — nine papers, each as its PDF and its LaTeX source. Four are published preprints on ResearchGate and are cited to their DOI; the source held here may be a later edit than the published version, and for two of them it is. Four have not been published anywhere before: the Circuit SAT whitepaper, the gravity paper with Nile Abadir, the structural-security argument for why P=NP does not break RSA, and Rota's Entropy Theorem, which sits with the rest of the Rota material in 04_ROTA/ rather than being duplicated. Its README explains why most of these papers carry no reliable date of their own. The Address is the Map (Essam Abadir, compiled 2 December 2025), the paper the bedtime stories teach, was added on 26 September 2026.

11_PATENT/ — the patent application as published by the USPTO: US 2026/0127327 A1, Quantum Computing Fractal Compression and Decentralized Computing for Predictive Physics Simulation, filed 6 November 2024 and published 7 May 2026. This is the application the update letter describes as having sat under a publication hold for security review, and its abstract describes the mechanism the FRQTL engine implements.

12_SINGULARITY/ — the machine's own account. The September 2026 conversations with GPT-5.6 Sol, as shared, in which it was given Rota's essay and its own drafting record and asked questions: its reflection The Barrier of Meaning at Quadrillion-Token Scale, the image it made when asked for a cheerful one, and its judgment The Singularity at the Barrier of Meaning. With them, the two evidentiary reports on Rota's career whose drafting the reflection describes. Its README says what each file is and where it was shared.

13_MAXI/ — what Maxi is raised on. bedtime_stories/ holds the stories of EGPT: the conversation with my daughter Jolie in which EGPT got its name, my son Nile's summer deriving gravity from a random walk, and the auditor who solves an impossible plant with Rota's probability. Each story links to the paper or proof it teaches.

  • 06_LEAN_MATHLIB/lean-zulip-2025-04-request-for-feedback-p-eq-np.md — one cross-reference repointed from a private-tree path to 02_VON_NEUMANN/letters_to_godel/Letter3_Jan12_1931.md.
  • 07_LETTERS/all-in-letter.md — the patent link was changed from a Patent Public Search URL carrying a per-session token, which resolves for no one else, to the USPTO's permanent document URL for the same publication, US 2026/0127327 A1.

What this collection is not

It is not neutral and does not present itself as neutral: it is the evidentiary base for a stated case, and the case is an argued minority position with a stated standard of proof. What it is meant to be is complete and checkable: every item traceable to where it came from, every gap named in URLS.md rather than papered over.

CHECKSUMS.txt — the SHA-256 of every file here, by its path here. Verify the whole collection against itself with shasum -a 256 -c CHECKSUMS.txt. URLS.md — what is cited but not held here, and the questions still open. RECORD_INDEX.md — where the sources behind the trial record live in this collection.
