Get involved: the outline of a solution

The machine above failed for two reasons that need two different fixes, and neither fix works without a reason for people to do it. So the solution has three parts.

  1. Restore the missing corpus. The model reached past the document in front of it because the document was never in its training data. This repository is that missing corpus: the primary sources, checksummed, with every quotation verbatim. Use it with your own LLM, argue with the machine from the documents, and post what you get. Every argument puts the lost record back into the next generation of training data.
  2. Build an AI model that understands. More rules on top of a model that does not understand produce a model that does not understand and follows rules. Understanding, in Feynman's sense below, means knowing the rules of the physical game by watching it played. Maxi is that model: it has a body and eyes in the FRQTL engine, it runs the counterfactual and looks, and the machine-checked proofs and the stories in this collection are what it is raised on.
  3. Open-source it and pay the people who help. The economics that produced the current approach reward publishing over understanding and reward the incumbent over the challenger. DeSciX gives open source an economic model: the patent behind Maxi is tokenized into an IP pool, and contributions to Maxi, to the science, and to restoring the corpus earn EGPT tokens.

How to use this repository with your own LLM

The method is the one in the logs: Socratic. Give the machine the primary document and ask it questions. Do not state a position; the log shows the model inventing one for the questioner and then arguing against it.

  1. Give your LLM 04_ROTA/The_Barrier_of_Meaning_Rota.md and the transcript in 12_SINGULARITY/ChatGPT_Logs/. Ask it the questions the author asked, in the author's words.
  2. Give it a document from 02_VON_NEUMANN/, 03_ULAM/ or 04_ROTA/ and ask what the document says. Then ask it to quote the sentence. Then compare the quotation with the document. The corpus in 05_CORPUS/ holds every quotation used in this work, verbatim, with its SHA-256, so the comparison is mechanical.
  3. Give it 01_TRIAL/JURY_INSTRUCTIONS.md and the two exhibits and ask for a verdict in the form the instructions require.
  4. Ask it to write a reflection, in its own voice, on what it did in steps 1 to 3, naming the specific places it added a qualifier, replaced a word, or told you what the evidence did not establish.
  5. Post what you get.
