10_PAPERS — the papers

Nine papers. Four are published preprints on ResearchGate and are cited here to their published record; four have not been published anywhere before and appear here for the first time. Every one is held as its PDF and its LaTeX source, so a reader can check the rendered document against what produced it.

Published: cite these to ResearchGate

The link is the published version. The source in this folder may be a later edit, and for two of them it demonstrably is: the PDF held here was compiled months after the version ResearchGate carries, and the titles differ. Where the two disagree, the ResearchGate record is what was published on that date, and the source here is where the paper went afterwards.

Dates, and why most of these papers do not carry one

Six of the nine sources set the date with the LaTeX command that means today. The date on the title page is therefore whenever the PDF was last compiled, not when the paper was written. There is no date in the source to recover. The table above gives the compile date of the PDF held here, taken from the PDF itself, and that is the closest thing to a date each paper has.

Two PDFs in this folder were compiled on 16 September 2026, because no compiled version existed:

  • Without_Attraction_There_Is_Nothing.pdf — built with pdflatex from the source and images in the same directory. The source sets no date, so the title page shows none.
  • Structural_Security_Of_Crypto_When_PeqNP.pdf — built with xelatex. Its source used to date itself to the compile day; it now states 4 March 2026, the date the source first entered the author's repository, so the paper carries a fixed date instead of a moving one. The source also asks for the Noto Sans typeface, which was not installed on the machine that compiled it, so the default roman font was used. The text is the source's own and unaltered. Recompiling with Noto Sans present will change how it looks and nothing it says.

Everything else here is byte-identical to the author's working copy, and CHECKSUMS.txt at the root of the collection carries the hash of every file.

Building any of them

Each paper's directory holds what it needs. From inside one:

latexmk -pdf <paper>.tex      # or -xelatex for AddressMap_And_Crypto

Two corrections made to the sources

Both were made in the author's working copy, not only in this published copy, so the two agree and the fix reaches anyone who builds from either.

  • The Circuit SAT whitepaper carried the author's contact email on its title page. It is removed from the source and the PDF here is rebuilt without it.
  • The structural-security paper dated itself to whatever day it was compiled. It is now dated 4 March 2026, the day its source first entered the author's repository.
