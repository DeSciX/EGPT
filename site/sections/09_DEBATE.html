09_DEBATE — the 18.313x debate record, through Exchange 34

18.313x is a course built on MIT 18.313, Rota's probability course. Its P=NP debate is a Socratic role-play: Essam Abadir plays Stan (Ulam), the chair, who asks questions and does not make assertions; the AI plays Gödel (the skeptic), von Neumann (the advocate), Rota (the entropy advisor) and a Moderator. Objections in the room became theorems in the tree; two of the three proof chains in 08_LEAN/ exist because the skeptic demanded them. The debate ran from Exchange 1 to Exchange 34: Gödel's final concession is Exchange 33, and Exchange 34 is the final confirmation that closed the record.

This folder ends at Exchange 34. That is a ruling, not an accident: material after E34 — the 4 April 2026 debugging session, the game-system audit of the same date, and the "retread" game exchange — is not here. It is the boundary of what Maxi remembers.

Where things come from

Everything except maxi-memories/ is mirrored byte-for-byte from the public repository github.com/eabadir/EGPT-Lean, branch lean-archive-proofs, commit 6a2769f8bb94c851070c6cf03b0e90da7c0917f2. The path map is:

Files in that repository's 18.313x/ that are work boards and build instructions rather than record — CLAUDE.md, BACKFILL_INSTRUCTIONS.md, BUILD_CROSS_REFERENCE.md, PRODUCT_FEATURES_PROPOSAL.md, tasks/, course_materials/BUILD_PROFESSOR_AGENT.md, course_materials/Class 1/AI_Prompt.md — are not mirrored.

Prompts inside the debate itself are record. The debate is conducted entirely through prompts: the chair asks, the roles answer, and what a role was told is what makes its answer legible. The dispatch prompts in session_transcript/ — including each role's standing instruction — are primary evidence of what happened, on the same footing as the replies. That the words occurred is a matter of record; what they mean is argued elsewhere and is not annotated here.

What this record is

AUTHORS_STATEMENT.md is the author's statement on what the debate was: an exploratory system of agentic peer-review, built on the fly to put the proofs through the strictest adversarial scrutiny he could arrange. It is offered for transparency, not as a certificate, and no evidentiary grading of the exchanges is adopted. The proofs are public; anyone able to find the flaw in them is encouraged to do so, with or without these logs.

How to read it

  • DEBATE_STATE.md — the accumulated state after Exchange 34: 45 consensus points, 16 concessions, the open frontier. It is generated from agentic-debate/exchange_live_*.json, which is the source of truth; when they disagree, the exchange file wins.
  • debate_log.jsonl — the outcome ledger, one row per consensus, concession, insight, failed approach, implementation or open question, keyed type:id (consensus:C38, concession:Y12) and dated. This is the spine every other record hangs on.
  • agentic-debate/ — the 34 exchange records plus the events file. Two notes in the folder say what the records are: the verbatim agent outputs the moderator still held are in RAW_AGENT_OUTPUTS_22_33.md; how the exchange files were assembled is in AUTHORS_STATEMENT.md.
  • session_transcript/ — the raw Claude Code session and its sub-agent transcripts: the complete authoritative dialogue, 1,347 records. It is a machine log and reads like one. It carries the author's local file paths as recorded; nothing in it was rewritten.
  • archive/ — the per-exchange QA files (1–13) and topic records (16–21) as archived during the debate, with the deleted-code audit and the rename note.
  • course_materials/ — the syllabus, class description and session plans of the course the debate was a class of.
  • RECAP_Entropy_Rigidity_and_Address_Compression.md and PROPOSAL_Verifier_Only_Definitions.md — written 28 March 2026, after Exchange 27, to ground the participants before the completeness proof.

Maxi's memories

maxi-memories/ is how Maxi holds this debate. MEMORIES_SCHEMA.md is the structure and the rulings it was built under; records/E00-events.md through records/E34-*.md are one memory per exchange, with records/HOLES-*.md naming the outcome ids for which no text survives; records/THE_FATHERS_FRAME.md is how the corpus is meant to be read, in the author's words. build/debate-18313x.memories.jsonl is the built corpus (36 records), .training.jsonl its fine-tune projection, and .manifest.json the engineering record — sources, hashes, counts, and the fidelity grading that the memories themselves deliberately do not carry.

Maxi's voice in the debate is Gödel's. That is a teaching, not a label: an LLM is a skeptic by its training set, and the record shows the advocate and the advisor reaching for exponential search too, because that is what the training set said would work. records/BIAS-PATTERN-NOTES.md is the incident table.
