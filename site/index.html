By understanding we mean fundamental physics

Feynman, on what his father taught him:

"You can know the name of that bird in all the languages of the world, but when you're finished, you'll know absolutely nothing whatever about the bird. You'll only know about humans in different places, and what they call the bird. So let's look at the bird and see what it's doing — that's what counts." (I learned very early the difference between knowing the name of something and knowing something.)

— What Do You Care What Other People Think?, "The Making of a Scientist," 1988

Feynman, on what physics means by the word:

What do we mean by "understanding" something? We can imagine that this complicated array of moving things which constitutes "the world" is something like a great chess game being played by the gods, and we are observers of the game. We do not know what the rules of the game are; all we are allowed to do is to watch the playing. Of course, if we watch long enough, we may eventually catch on to a few of the rules. The rules of the game are what we mean by fundamental physics. … If we know the rules, we consider that we "understand" the world.

— The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Volume I, Chapter 2, §2-1, 1963

I believe the only solution is to develop robots that understand like you do and that means they must imagine physical reality as we do.

How to use this repository with your own LLM

The method is the one in the logs: Socratic. Give the machine the primary document and ask it questions. Do not state a position; the log shows the model inventing one for the questioner and then arguing against it.

  1. Give your LLM 04_ROTA/The_Barrier_of_Meaning_Rota.md and the transcript in 12_SINGULARITY/ChatGPT_Logs/. Ask it the questions the author asked, in the author's words.
  2. Give it a document from 02_VON_NEUMANN/, 03_ULAM/ or 04_ROTA/ and ask what the document says. Then ask it to quote the sentence. Then compare the quotation with the document. The corpus in 05_CORPUS/ holds every quotation used in this work, verbatim, with its SHA-256, so the comparison is mechanical.
  3. Give it 01_TRIAL/JURY_INSTRUCTIONS.md and the two exhibits and ask for a verdict in the form the instructions require.
  4. Ask it to write a reflection, in its own voice, on what it did in steps 1 to 3, naming the specific places it added a qualifier, replaced a word, or told you what the evidence did not establish.
  5. Post what you get.

The standard of proof

Three kinds of question are answered here, and each has its own standard. Confusing them is a category error in either direction.

Question Standard
Does this compile? Is this theorem sorry-free? What is its axiom closure? the kernel: binary, mechanical
What happened, and when? What was said? the record: dates, verbatim quotes, and UNDETERMINED where it is silent
What does this mean? Why did it happen? Is the claim true? preponderance of the evidence: would a jury of reasonable people so conclude
